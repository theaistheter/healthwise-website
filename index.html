General Information

ABOUT

What is HealthWise?

HealthWise is a school-based academic website created to demonstrate web design, responsive layout, health-information presentation, and custom form validation.

The intended audience is students, teachers, and other school community members who want basic, reliable health information.

Important:

This website is for general educational information only. It is not a substitute for advice, diagnosis, or treatment from a qualified health professional.

These topics provide general information from trustworthy health organizations.

Hydration

Water is important for normal body functions. Needs vary depending on factors such as activity, environment, age, and health.

😴

Healthy Sleep

Getting enough sleep supports physical health, emotional well-being, attention, and everyday functioning. Recommended sleep amounts vary by age.

Learn from CDC ↗
🏃

Physical Activity

Regular physical activity can support heart health, physical fitness, and overall well-being. Choose activities that are appropriate for your age and abilities.

Learn from WHO ↗
🧼

Hand Hygiene

Washing hands with soap and water helps remove germs and can reduce the spread of respiratory and other infections.
